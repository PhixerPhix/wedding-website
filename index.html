Where

Cote House, Cote Drive Bristol BS9 3UR

For most of the day we will be in or around the marquee. It will be heated, but it's in a field. The flooring may be uneven and the grass outside bumpy or muddy. So do wear comfortable shoes.

Travel/Parking: We’d be really grateful if you would try to arrive and leave quietly and on foot if at all possible!!

There is no parking on site and we have some very sensitive neighbours who we are keen not to upset. Contact us in advance for disabled parking.

Taxis: For the same reason we’d like to try to avoid taxis going all over the place. If you need a taxi, please try to arrange drop-off or pick-up opposite 1 Cote House Lane, BS9 3UW
